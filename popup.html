Lyra

v0.1.2

Floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mini player with real-time synced lyrics for YouTube Music.

How to Use

  1. Open YouTube Music ↗
  2. Play any song of your choice.
  3. Switch to another tab to automatically activate the Mini Player!
